Add UniPulse to Your Home Screen

UniPulse is configured with a Web App Manifest, custom app icon, and offline storage (`localStorage` + `IndexedDB`) so it runs full-screen like a native app.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):
  1. Open this standalone HTML file or hosted link in **Safari**.
  2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
On Android (Chrome) or Desktop:
  1. Tap the three-dot browser menu in **Chrome**.
  2. Select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
